THE CHRONICLE

Synthesizing Heritage & Geometric Purity

D–SKETCH began with a single vision: to honor the warm, vernacular soul of Kerala’s timbered courtyard architecture while integrating the geometric precision of international modernism. From our roots in Mongam, Malappuram, we have spent over 12 years elevating residential and commercial landscapes.

Our designs are highly inspired by the "Nalukettu" courtyard philosophy—focusing heavily on open central voids, natural light flow, tropical breeze cycles, and rain water features—choreographed under a sleek, minimal, high-end design lens. We curate local teak woods, custom brass inlays, and raw concrete surfaces to formulate a distinct visual voice.

Our Mission
